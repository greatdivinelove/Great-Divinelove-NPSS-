ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

Welcome to Great DivineLove

Great DivineLove Nursery, Primary and Secondary School is a vision-driven school established with a deep sense of purpose and a passion for raising children who are academically sound, morally grounded, confident and prepared for the future.

We believe that every child carries a unique gift. Our aim is to provide a caring and stimulating environment where every learner can discover, develop and confidently use their gifts.

Our foundation is built on wisdom, excellence, discipline, respect, creativity and love for every child.
